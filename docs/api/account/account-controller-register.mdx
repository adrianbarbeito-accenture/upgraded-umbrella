**Tag:** `Account` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Operation:** `AccountController_register`

## Purpose

Creates a new account so a prospective member can join the community. The account starts in a pending state and is not usable until the member confirms their email address through the activation flow — this two-step shape keeps the platform free of accounts owned by addresses no one actually controls. On success the system queues the verification email automatically so the member's next action is to check their inbox.
