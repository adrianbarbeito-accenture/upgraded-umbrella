## Purpose

Public landing experience shown to anyone who hasn't signed in. It introduces Esmorga in one or two sentences and offers two clear next steps: sign in with an existing account, or register a new one. Any deeper interaction with the product — joining events, voting, creating events — requires a member to first take one of these two paths.
