## Purpose

Entry point of the forgotten-password flow. A member who can't remember their password types the email they registered with; the system replies with a confirmation that an email has been sent (whether or not the address matches a real account, by design) and the member is told to check their inbox for a one-time code. They then move on to the reset-password screen with that code in hand.
